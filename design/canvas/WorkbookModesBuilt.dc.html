Built 6 parts, TransferCase_Parallel_Layout and TransferCase_Parallel.SLDASM; no interference between the gears in any mode, where they stand. Its gears are mated to turn in each of its 4 modes, one configuration each (2H, 4H, N and 4L), and SolidWorks reports no mate in error in any of them: it opens in 2H; to switch mode, double-click another mode's configuration in SolidWorks' ConfigurationManager. Drag InPin in SolidWorks to turn one train and FrontDog to turn the other. RangeSlv and ModeSlv slide along Rear, each to its place in each mode. A turn you give the gears in one mode shows in every mode, so a dog can stand across its sleeve in another mode; to put the gears back there, press Update SolidWorks with that mode showing, since an Update pressed in another mode may leave them as they stand. 1 thing to do by hand: Assign a material to each of the 6 parts; the build leaves it unset.
