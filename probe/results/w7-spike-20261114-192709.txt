Summary of what was measured; the raw run follows below, and the JSON is w7-spike-20261114-192709.json.
- Question: a gear mate between a sun on a fixed axis and a planet whose axis rides a turning carrier.
- Answer: SolidWorks measures the gear mate in the carrier's frame (the frame of the line joining the two axes), not the
  fixed frame. With the sun held by its drive and the carrier driven +30, a single sun-planet 18:18 gear mate turned
  the planet +60 in the fixed frame (+30 relative to the carrier). The fixed-frame model would give 0 here, and Willis gives +60.
- W7-PLAN J5's mates (sun-planet for each of 3 copies, one planet-ring mate, ring fixed, carrier free on its axis):
  after the read-back fix (the planet-ring mate needed Flip True), a sun drive of +30 gave:
  - carrier +7.5, each planet -15 in the fixed frame (-22.5 relative to the carrier), ring 0, every gear turning as the
    kinematics say;
  - status 1 on all four gear mates, no mate error, no interference at the driven position across the six meshes
    (including the two unmated planet-ring meshes);
  - the same motion after save, close and reopen.
- Read-back (decide.read_back_says_right) matched the drive for both kinds. The sun-planet mates read right with
  Flip False and were right. The planet-ring mate read wrong with Flip False, and driven that way it was wrong
  (carrier -15, planets -60, 5 clashes each ring mesh); remade with Flip True it read right and was right.
- One planet against three: the same 0.11 s rebuild and drive. Adding the other planets' sun mates did not over-define
  anything (statuses 1, no errors).
- The plan's section 2.9 fallback (every body gear-mated to the sun at its solved absolute speed ratio) turns the
  planets WRONG: -3.75 against -15, with clashes, because a planet's mate is measured relative to the carrier. It must
  not be used.
