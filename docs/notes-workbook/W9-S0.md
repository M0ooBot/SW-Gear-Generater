# W9 S0: the user's sprocket on a gear Update puts back, the grounded tie, and What's Wrong

The questions are ISSUE-1-PLAN §4's S0 and P0, as revised after the second review (S5): which route K2 takes when
Update puts a train back to its built frames while a user's component is locked to one of its gears, (a) the put
carries the component and nothing of the user's is written, or (b) the user's mates on the train are suppressed
around the put and turned back on; what the read before a press says when a user mate ties a gear to something
fixed; and whether a failing rebuild opens What's Wrong.

The evidence is one run of `probe/w9_user_work_check.py` on SolidWorks 2026 (34.1.1), in a fresh session, from a git
archive of 55a82c1: `probe/results/w9-s0-sw2026-20261116-050200.log` and `w9-s0-20261116-045653.json` (92a45fe).
Every number below is from them. **There is no 2024 run.** The 2026 run met the brief's stop condition (neither route
keeps the sprocket on its gear), so the run on 2024 waits for the coordinator's ruling. Nothing goes into
`findings.py`. The proposed findings are at the end.

## P0: What's Wrong

"Show errors every rebuild" (`swUserPreferenceToggle_e` 77) was **on** as found and stayed on. The setup was a
scratch strip gear with a user keyway: a Ø3 cut-extrude through the bore's edge, made by the session's own sketch and
cut calls. Bore Diameter went 10 → 14, which left the keyway inside the bore. Then:

- the keyway read `GetErrorCode2` **71** (`ICE`, warning flag False);
- the part's rebuild returned **False**;
- `GetWhatsWrongCount` read **1**;
- **no window** of SolidWorks' process came up in the 8 s after.

So a rebuild through the API that fails on a user feature **does not open What's Wrong** on 2026. The failure is
reported only by the rebuild's return, the feature's code and the count. This also answers M3 in part: a part
rebuild with only a user feature in error returns False.

## The puts (cases 0, A and B)

The angles below are each body's turn about its own axis, in degrees, from the JSON's frames. In every case and mode
the put took every app body to its built frame to 6.6e-10, with the app's mates back on. In every case, no mate read
an error code, every flag read back as set in every configuration, and no read marked a document.

| Case | Route | H: drive (Sun, sprocket) | H: after the put (Sun, sprocket) | L: after the drive | L: after the put |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (control, no lock) | — | Sun 37, both sprockets 0 | Sun 0, sprockets 0 | Sun 37, sprockets 0 | Sun 0, sprockets 0 |
| A-a: Sprocket-1 locked to Sun | (a), lock on | 37, 37 | **0, 37** | 37, 74 | **0, 74** |
| A-b | (b), lock suppressed in the mode and back on | 37, 37 | **0, 37** | 37, 74 | **0, 74** |
| B-a: Sprocket-2 locked to RangeSlv | (a) | RangeSlv 47.59, sprocket 37 | **RangeSlv 10.59, sprocket 37** | RangeSlv 23.36, sprocket 49.77 | **10.59, 49.77** |
| B-b | (b) | 47.59, 37 | **10.59, 37** | 23.36, 49.77 | **10.59, 49.77** |

RangeSlv's built turn is 10.59° in both modes. Sprocket-2 slid with RangeSlv from z −70 in H to −60 in L, so the lock
held along the shaft across modes.

**Neither route carries the sprocket.**

- **The drive.** The locked sprocket turned with its gear exactly: its pose in the gear's frame was unchanged to
  2e-16.
- **Route (a).** The put moved the gear back, the sprocket stayed where the drive left it, and the lock took the new
  offset with code 0.
- **Route (b).** It reads the same to the last digit: flags back on in every configuration, code 0, and the sprocket
  left at the drive's angle.

This is `findings.LOCK_RETAKES_AN_OFFSET` (P2, `lock_retake_offset`): a lock re-takes whatever relative frame it
finds. It holds for a lock that stays on through a `Transform2` put of one of its components and its rebuild, as well
as for one turned back on. Each further put adds to the offset: 74° after H's and L's puts. Nothing in SolidWorks
reports it: no code, no flag, no constrained status (Sprocket-1 read 2 throughout).

So by the plan's own test, the sprocket is not "found on its gear afterwards" (W9a's *Done when*). Route (b) comes
back clean in flags and codes but not in position. The script's verdict was plan §4's STOP "a put that holds in
neither route". Read precisely: every put held, and neither route kept the user's component where it was relative to
its gear. Whether that is acceptable, or calls for something neither route is (for example, putting the user's
locked component with its gear), is the coordinator's and the user's question. No third route was tried.

The control behaved as a control. In case 0, the sprockets mated only to the layout's axes stood still under every
drive and put. In A, Sprocket-2, not locked, stood still while Sprocket-1 turned; in B, the other way round.

## The tie (cases C and C2)

- **C** (Sprocket-1 locked to Sun, its Top plane coincident with the fixed Housing-1's): before any put, every body
  of Sun's train read `GetConstrainedStatus` **3** (fully defined). That was so unshown in H, shown in H and shown
  in L: Sun, the three planet copies, the carrier, HighDog, LowDog and RangeSlv, 3 each, and Sprocket-1 3. Every
  other case read 2 for every mated body. The read in the showing mode found the tie.
- **The drive against the tie** turned nothing (Sun 0°) and the drive job saw no error.
- **A put of Sun alone +37° against the tie** (the app's route mates off) was **taken**. Sun stood at +37 with the
  mates back on, no mate in error, and the tie and the housing unmoved: the user's lock re-took that offset too. The
  put back to the built frames then took Sun to 0. So the tie does not stop a put. Refusing before the press, as K2
  says, is the app's only defence.
- **C2** (the tie suppressed in H and N, on in L): the unshown read with H active said free (every body 2), and so
  did H shown. L shown said tied (every body 3). **The showing mode's read does not decide C2**; L must be read when
  shown, as K2's last clause says.
- **C2 and another mode.** In H, the put left Sun's lock at a −37° offset, because Sprocket-1 stayed at +37 there.
  When L was then shown, the tie held Sprocket-1 at 0, so the lock put **Sun at −37° from built in L**, and RangeSlv
  with it (−2.19 against 10.59), with no error code. A re-taken user lock thus moves another mode's train. L's own
  put then took everything back to built.

## Other readings

- **`AddComponent5` into the reopened assembly with modes** (first use; ahead of W9c's M5): Sprocket-1, Sprocket-2
  and Housing-1 each read `GetSuppression2` 2 (fully resolved) in H, N and L when shown. Housing-1, fixed once with
  H active, read `IsFixed` True in all three. Nothing had to be set per configuration.
- **New mates made with H active**: every one (axis, lock, plane coincident; `AddMate5` status 1) read **on in every
  configuration as made**. That is not the "suppressed in some configurations" API-LEDGER:220 records for a mate made
  while a mode is active. The script set and read back every flag all the same.
- **GetSaveFlag.** It was read around every read (frames, constrained status, mate codes, `IsSuppressed2` per
  configuration), 9 to 11 reads a case, and none marked a document. The saved, reopened assembly read unmarked
  straight after the open.
- **Modes.** In every case, L shown after H's drive and put read every body at L's built frame before L's drive.
  Either H's drive turn did not reach L here (unlike W8 s2 and s7 on E4), or H's put carried to L; the run does not
  separate the two. The last read after L's put had every body at its built frame in H, N and L (6.6e-10).

## Proposed findings (for the review; not in `findings.py`)

- `USER_MATE_PUT_ROUTE` (2026): **neither "carried" nor "suppress around"**. The value waits for the coordinator's
  ruling. Evidence: w9-s0-sw2026-20261116-050200.log, A-a / A-b / B-a / B-b, "the put back to the built frames in
  H" and "in L". Each `step` line shows errors [] and every body at its target to 6.6e-10; each following line shows
  the locked sprocket's pose in its gear's frame off by 0.60 (H) and 0.52 / 0.19 (L) against before the drive. The
  JSON's frames: Sprocket-1 37° then 74° while Sun is at 0. If a value is wanted that says what SolidWorks does:
  `USER_LOCK_RETAKES_PUT_OFFSET_ON_2026 = True`, for both routes.
- `USER_TIE_READ` (2026): **`GetConstrainedStatus` 3 on every body of the tied train** (never 4 here), read in the
  showing mode. It decides every mode only when the user's mates have one flag in every mode; C2's L had to be shown.
  Evidence: the log's `propose USER_TIE_READ` line, and `ok S0 C2: in H the train reads free` / `in L the train
  reads not free`.
- `WHATS_WRONG_OPENS_ON_API_REBUILD_ON_2026 = False`. Evidence: the log's P0 lines 9-11: the toggle on, the
  keyway's code 71, rebuild False, GetWhatsWrongCount 1, no new window.
