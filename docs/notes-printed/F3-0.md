## Decisions the plan left open, each the coordinator's to rule

1. **Two notes**, `F3-INTERFACES.md` and this one, as F1-0 and F2-0.
2. **The default is not in the geometry at F3-0.** No gear writes `"Bore Allowance"` until A does: putting it in at F3-0
   would have needed B's `update._part` and C's `_update_target` threaded too for the suite to pass, and the plan gives
   those to B and C. The run "with the default stubbed in" was made in a scratch copy instead (above). **Consequence:**
   once A's global lands, six of F3-0's pins and four existing tests are red until B's and C's threading is in; they are
   guards and nobody edits them. The order of landing, or one suite run after all three, is the coordinator's.
3. **`as_built(spec, ())` reads "0"**, by the plan's rule for a `variables` given (the re-check's point 3). The build
   journal's entries hold no globals and `_Parts.find` matches them with `()` (`gearbox.py:606`), so a journaled part F3
   built at 0.2 would read as 0 and could be reused under the wrong figure. Recommended to B: write each journal entry's
   `variables` (the job's) and pass them, or not reuse a journaled printed part with a blank allowance.
4. **`built_allowance` takes part in equality**, and `with_values` keeps it unless changed.
5. **`BORE_ALLOWANCE_DEFAULT` lives in `spec.py`** (spec cannot import the gears); `spur.BORE_ALLOWANCE_DEFAULT` is it, and the
   Gears column's note is written from it, so V1 is one constant and its pins.
6. **Names beyond the plan's**: `spec.DRIVE_ROUND`, `DRIVE_FLAT`, `DRIVES`, `BORE_FIELDS`, `RECORD_ONLY_FIELDS`,
   `BORE_ALLOWANCE_GLOBAL`, `BORE_FLAT_GLOBAL`, `GearSpec.has_flat()`, `bore_as_cut_mm()`; the derived fields named
   `bore_allowance` and `bore_flat`, with a `bore_as_cut` property; `store.BORE_GLOBALS`; `schema.DRIVE_WORDS`;
   `gearbox.as_built_job` as the per-candidate match's piece (`_matches` keeps its signature); `printout.BoreFigures` and
   `bore_figures`.
7. **What "at its default" is in `to_dict`**: Drive blank or round in any case; Bore allowance and Flat blank. A typed 0
   allowance is kept. A Flat typed with Drive round is kept too (A's to warn about or not).
8. **`sidecar_schema` gives 4 whenever the globals hold either name, whatever the spec**, machined included.
9. **The fixtures**: RangeBox's rows have no bore, so the RangeBox the plan names is RangeBox_Fixed with its Sun, Planet,
   dogs and Hub bored, printed and machined; with them the printed 12 : 1 and four parts from the form, Coupon_C among
   them.
10. **The print list's pin is the whole part block**, not the word *bore 10*: a part with no allowance and a round bore
    keeps *… · bore 10 mm · …* exactly, which is the plan's *bore 8 for a part cut at 8* in today's words.
11. **The strip's contract (F3-INTERFACES §5)**: the derive while a record is open is through `as_built`, and
    `_update_target` applies it itself to the form's spec. Open for C: a **Build** from the form while a record is open
    makes a part not on record, so it should take the default, not the opened part's figure.
12. **The card pin binds C**: *Bore as cut* and the three explainers must not appear on a machined gear with no
    allowance and a round bore; the explainers are kept out of `EXPLAINER_FIELDS`, as the span's card is.
13. **`probe/f1_demonstration.py`**: its link literal is split as F3-INTERFACES §12 says. Its printed sidecar judgement (3)
