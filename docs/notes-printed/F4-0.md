## Each formula, its source, and what could not be verified

The standard (ISO 6336-3:2006, :2019) was not read: it is sold, and no copy was to be had. Everything below rests on
secondary sources and on the reviewers' and F4-0's own working; **checked against the standard itself: nothing.**

| What | As built | Source | Verified how |
|---|---|---|---|
| σF = Ft / (b mn) YF YS, the load at the tip, Y_ε = Y_β = 1, every K 1 | §5.1 | ISO 6336-3 §6 as MITcalc gives it | MITcalc's page re-read for F4-0 (2026-11-19): YF, YS, L and qs exactly as built |
| YF = 6 (hF/mn) cos αF / ((sFn/mn)² cos αn) | `_section_rating` | MITcalc | the review derived it from beam statics; the tests check it against a beam calculation on the drawn tooth (four gears, 1e-9) |
| YS = (1.2 + 0.13 L) qs^(1/(1.21 + 2.3/L)), L = sFn/hF, qs = sFn/(2ρF) | `stress_correction` | MITcalc | the formula only; its range 1 ≤ qs < 8 is from search results quoting the 2006 text (not on MITcalc's page) |
| the 30° tangent outside, 60° on a ring | `TANGENT_OUTSIDE`, `TANGENT_RING` | MITcalc's T = π/3 and π/6 in its chord formulas (the tangent at 90° − T to the centreline); search results quoting the 2006 text | consistent with ISO's own chord formula; the review's Method B coding gives the chart values at z 20 |

**What A's note says (the review's S4), each figure the review's and, where marked, reproduced by F4-0's tests:**

- **The app's tooth against ISO's generated tooth.** Unshifted, with no backlash, the app's YF is 3.326, 2.755 and
  2.546 at module 2 and 20, 40 and 100 teeth, and 3.696 at module 1 and 20 teeth (depth 2.4 m): **pinned**. ISO's own
  generated tooth, loaded at the tip with the 1.25 / 0.38 rack, gives 2.800, 2.405, 2.195 (the review's coding of
  Method B, not reproduced here). So the app's tooth reads **15 to 32 % weaker in YF** (18 to 38 % with YS): its root,
  a radial line under the base circle and a fillet set on it, is narrower than a trochoid (sFn/m 1.801 at z 20 against
  ISO's 1.945). That is the tooth as drawn and printed, which is the plan's point.
- **The 30° section is ISO's convention, not the beam's worst.** Scanning the drawn fillet, the worst section reads
  above the 30° one by 5.2, 3.6, 9.2 and 2.5 % in YF (A1, A2, A8, m 2 z 100), and 3.9, 2.6, 7.4 and 1.7 % with YS
  taken at each point: **pinned**. So the figure can read up to about 7 % low on small pinions; *exact* in §5.1 is
  about the geometry only.
- **The normal-section fillet.** The fillet is drawn in the transverse section, so in the normal section its radius is
  between ρ cos² β and ρ / cos β; keeping ρ moves YS by at most about 3 % at β 20°.
- **Layers.** On a helical tooth the root's bending stress leaves the layer plane by sin 30° · sin β, about 0.17 σF of
  shear between layers at β 20° (0.21 at 25°), against a printed PLA's 31 MPa across layers and 35 within (by search
  summaries). No derating (H11), and every sentence says the figure is for teeth printed flat.
- **Sources read:** MITcalc's ISO 6336 page (re-read for F4-0). Not read: the standard, AGMA 2001, IEC 61400-4,
  AGMA 6123, Bambu's data sheet.

## Decisions the plan left open, each the coordinator's to rule

1. **Two notes**, `F4-INTERFACES.md` and this one, as F1-0 to F3-0.
2. **`ToothRating` gains `short` and `ring`** past §5.6's list: the reason in a few words (the form's row and the
   Results list need one), and whether the teeth are a ring's (the words say *ring* and its fillet clause). `why` is a
   clause naming no gear, for the caller to prefix.
3. **A helical ring is not rated when its virtual ring or its drawn section has its tips inside its base circle.** The
   plan names the virtual ring's test; in F4-0's sweep (and the re-check's) the two never disagree, so the *or* changes
   no answer, and it keeps the sentence's figures the drawn section's (the user's own diameters) whenever that section
   is the one below. Ruled the other way it is one line.
4. **`holds` raises `ValueError` on a rating not rated**, rather than return 0 or NaN that could be shown as a figure.
5. **`allowable_mpa` never raises**: a blank or anything that is not a number above zero is 17. The error is said once,
   at the Box cell, by `_read_box`.
6. **Three more *not rated* reasons than the plan names**, none met in the app's gears: an unknown recipe, an involute
   starting outside the outer diameter (the tip on the radial line), and anything not a spur, helical, herringbone or
   bevel derived gear.
7. **The Box's note** reads *the root stress a tooth is rated to take once*: §5.4's *rated to once* read as a slip.
8. **The Box's errors** are Nozzle's: *must be a number (got 'x')* for text, *must be more than zero (got '0')* for zero
   or less. §5.4's example put *more than zero* beside *'x'*.
9. **`schema` imports `gears.strength.ALLOWABLE_DEFAULT`** for the setting's default and note, so changing 17 is one
   constant and its pins (as F3 did for the bore allowance's default).
10. **The rounding** (§5.4, F4-0's to fix): forces whole from 10 N and to 0.1 N under; torques to 0.01 N·m under 10, 0.1
    under 100, whole from 100; times to 0.1; the allowable as few figures as it needs. One home in `gears.strength`
    (`newtons`, `newton_metres`, `times`, `megapascals`), so B and C round alike.
11. **Stubs beyond F4-0's file list**: `workbook/strength.py` (new, B's) and `ui_text.holds_row` at the file's end
    (C's), as F3-0 left stubs in B's and C's files. `checks.Report.strength` and the call in `check` are left to B, as
    §5.6 gives them.
12. **The words** (F4-INTERFACES §6) settle what §5.4 left as proposed: the ring's fillet clause after the ring's name;
    the unrated ring's sentence with the gear that rates named; the Results' Mode cell blank with no modes, the
    *Weaker gear* cell *Sun (Ring not rated)*, and the *Worst share* list in a third cell; the line under the form's
    rows prefixed *Holds once:*.
13. **The B-row test in F4-0's file** works §5.2's force rules out row by row, by hand. It does not build B's module and
    B pins the same rows through its own; it holds now that A's maths and the app's kinematics give every B figure.
14. **The plan's §5.2 opening** (*each Meshes row whose two gears are rated*): F4-INTERFACES §4 reads it as covering a
    mesh with one ring not rated, as the re-check asks. Changing the plan's sentence is the coordinator's.
